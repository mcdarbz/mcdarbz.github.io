Preface

Every person is a learner.

We learn from school, families, relationships, work, successes, failures, and everyday experiences.

Three Core Questions:

  • What makes information become understanding?
  • How does understanding become ability?
  • How does a person keep learning throughout life?

This primer is not to be believed — it is to be explored.

Part II — Understanding the Root

What Is Comprehension?

  • Knowing = information received
  • Comprehending = grasping meaning & relationships
  • Applying = using understanding in action

The Tree Analogy

Root → Trunk → Branches → Leaves → Fruit
Comprehension → Knowledge → Abilities → Skills → Contributions

The Learning Cycle

EXPERIENCE → OBSERVATION → QUESTION → CONNECTION →
COMPREHENSION → ABILITY → APPLICATION → FEEDBACK → NEW EXPERIENCE

Not rigid — we revisit, revise, and deepen throughout life.

Part IV — CRT in a Changing World

Human–AI Partnership

  • AI generates information — but information ≠ comprehension
  • AI expands the branches — analysis, ideas, assistance
  • Human comprehension remains the root — questioning, judging, verifying

AI handles information • Humans provide meaning

No one begins as an expert. Specialization grows from:

Every specialist began as a learner at their root.

Part V — Exploring the CRT

Your Reflection Guide

  • What do I know but not yet fully understand?
  • What experience taught me the most?
  • What mistake changed my thinking?
  • Am I solving the real problem — or only its symptoms?
  • When I use AI, do I understand and verify the answer?

Toward Research & Improvement

The CRT is open to investigation — not settled conclusion.

  • Does deliberate comprehension improve retention?
  • Does connecting new info to prior knowledge help transfer?
  • Can CRT-based methods improve real-world problem-solving?
  • How does comprehension develop across a lifetime?

The theory is shaped by evidence, testing, and open inquiry.
